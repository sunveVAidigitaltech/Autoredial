d. Long Description:
Auto Redial is the ultimate productivity tool for getting through to busy phone lines, customer service centers, or sold-out ticket hotlines. 

With Auto Redial, you can queue up a list of target numbers and the app will automatically cycle through them, calling each one seamlessly until someone picks up. 

Key Features:
- Smart Cycle Dialing: Enter multiple numbers (e.g., Doctor's office line 1, line 2, line 3) and the app will loop through them.
- Precision Delays: Set the exact delay between calls, anywhere from 1 second to 15 minutes.
- Auto Speakerphone: Automatically turn on the speakerphone the moment the call connects.
- Call History: Automatically logs every call session, showing you exactly how many attempts were made and to which numbers.
- Scheduled Calling: Need to be the first caller at 8:00 AM? Set a scheduled redial session and the app will wake up and start dialing exactly on time.

Stop wasting time manually tapping redial. Let Auto Redial do the hard work for you.
